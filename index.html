Mechanical Work

أعمال ميكانيكية

The core of what we do. Diagnosis, repair and routine maintenance for the systems that keep your car moving.

  • Engine Repair

    إصلاح المحرك

    From rough idling and warning lights to full overhauls. We diagnose the fault first, explain what it will take, and only then start work.

  • Oil & Filter Change

    تغيير الزيت والفلتر

    Engine oil, oil filter, air filter and cabin filter. We match the grade to your vehicle and log the mileage so you know when the next one is due.

  • Brake Service

    خدمة الفرامل

    Pads, discs, brake fluid and handbrake adjustment. If you hear grinding or the pedal feels soft, bring it in before it turns into an expensive job.

  • Suspension

    نظام التعليق

    Shock absorbers, bushes, ball joints and springs. Al Ain roads and speed bumps are hard on suspension, and worn parts wear out your tyres too.

  • Transmission

    ناقل الحركة

    Automatic and manual gearbox work, fluid service and clutch replacement. Slipping gears and delayed engagement are the signs to watch for.

  • AC Service

    خدمة التكييف

    Regas, compressor and condenser repair, and leak tracing. In an Al Ain summer a working air conditioner is not a comfort, it is a necessity.

  • Radiator & Cooling

    المبرد والتبريد

    Radiators, water pumps, thermostats and hoses. Overheating damages an engine quickly, so a rising temperature gauge is worth stopping for.

Electrical Work

أعمال كهربائية

Modern cars are as much wiring as they are metal. We trace faults properly instead of swapping parts and hoping.

  • Battery Service

    خدمة البطارية

    Testing, charging, terminal cleaning and replacement. If the car cranks slowly or needs a jump start, the battery is usually telling you something.

  • Alternator Repair

    إصلاح الدينمو

    Repair and replacement of the alternator and its belt. A battery that keeps going flat is often an alternator that has stopped charging it.

  • Starter Motor

    موتور التشغيل

    Starter motors and solenoids, repaired or replaced. A single click when you turn the key usually points here rather than at the battery.

  • Wiring Diagnostics

    تشخيص الأسلاك

    Fault tracing across the loom, fuses, relays and earth points. Intermittent electrical problems take patience, and we take the time to find them.

  • Headlights & Taillights

    المصابيح الأمامية والخلفية

    Bulbs, housings, indicators and wiring. We fix the connections behind them too, which is usually the real reason a light keeps failing.

Denting & Painting

سمكرة ودهان

Bodywork and paint, from a car park scrape to putting a vehicle back together after an accident.

  • Dent Removal

    إزالة الانبعاجات

    Pulling and reshaping panels back to their original line. Where the paint is still intact we work without repainting the panel at all.

  • Full Body Respray

    دهان كامل

    Complete resprays in a controlled spray booth. Panels are stripped, prepared and sprayed so the finish is even across the whole car.

  • Scratch Repair

    إصلاح الخدوش

    Light scuffs polished out. Deeper scratches are filled, primed and blended into the surrounding paint so the repair does not show.

  • Bumper Repair

    إصلاح المصد

    Cracks, splits and broken mounting points on plastic bumpers repaired rather than replaced wherever the part is still sound.

  • Color Matching

    مطابقة الألوان

    We mix to your vehicle's paint code and check the blend against the surrounding panels before anything is sprayed.

  • Accident Damage

    أضرار الحوادث

    Structural straightening, panel replacement and refinishing. We walk you through what needs doing before any work starts.
